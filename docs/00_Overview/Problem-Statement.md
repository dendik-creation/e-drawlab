# Problem Statement

## The educational problem, as stated

Neither approved document contains a section literally titled *Latar Belakang* or *Rumusan Masalah*. The problem is stated distributively across `DESKRIPSI UMUM BAHAN AJAR` and `RENCANA IMPLEMENTASI`, and is reconstructed here without addition.

> Source: Approved Proposal — Sections `DESKRIPSI UMUM BAHAN AJAR`, `RENCANA IMPLEMENTASI`

1. **Physical practicum is constrained by space and equipment.** The proposal positions the virtual lab as a way to experiment "tanpa batas ruang dan alat fisik" — without the limits of room and physical instruments.
2. **Errors in technical drawing are expensive downstream.** The trigger case is an assembly failure caused by a miscalculated dimensional tolerance; the closing rationale is saving practicum time, minimising acrylic mis-cuts and preventing damaged circuit material caused by structural miscalculation.
3. **Procedural competency is unverified before students touch materials.** The product exists to measure procedural competence *before* physical practicum, and completion is made a pre-requisite for workshop access.
4. **Standards compliance is abstract until it fails.** The lesson plan asks the teacher to reinforce why IEC/ANSI compliance matters "agar tidak memicu kegagalan produksi massal fisik" — so students meet the consequence of non-compliance inside the simulation instead of in production.
5. **Access is unreliable.** The distribution model assumes the file is shared via the school LMS or a chat group and then run offline on lab machines — so connectivity cannot be a precondition for learning (REQ-NF-003).

## What the revision adds to the problem framing

> Source: Approved Revision — Sections `STAGE 2`, `STAGE 3`

The revision reframes two stages from *rules to obey* into *quantities to compute*: trace width from load current and copper weight, casing size from board size, standoff, tallest component and wall thickness. The implied gap is that students can follow a drawing convention without being able to derive it — the revision's worked examples and distractor analysis ("Jebakan A terjadi jika siswa lupa mengalikan celah dengan 2 sisi") target exactly that arithmetic failure.

## What is *not* claimed

No prior-study statistics, no baseline test scores, no cited literature, no institution-level needs analysis appear in either document. This vault does not manufacture them. If the project needs an evidence base for a report, that is an open item — [[Open-Questions]].
